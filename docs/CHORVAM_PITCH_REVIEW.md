## Valuation wording

Estimated carcass kg = estimated live kg × carcass-yield fraction.
Indicative carcass value = estimated carcass kg × dated carcass price/kg.
Cattle's existing 50–58% scenarios are not measured individual yield.
Retail boneless-meat prices cannot automatically substitute for carcass prices.
Show a range/date/source. This is not a bank collateral limit or independent
whole-animal appraisal; breed, condition and production value also matter.
