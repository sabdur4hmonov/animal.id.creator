# Chorvam roadmap — 2026-11-12

These phases describe validation dependencies, not a claim that every phase is
finished in the field. Data-independent pilot modules were brought forward by
Sardorbek's later instructions. See [STATUS_FOR_PITCH.md](STATUS_FOR_PITCH.md)
for the feature-by-feature boundary and [PROGRESS.md](../PROGRESS.md) for checks.

## Phase 1 — Cattle collection and pilot foundation

Built: native camera/single-photo and four-second JPEG burst, real blur/exposure checks, offline L-ID profiles and image/measurement history, side-photo/scale collection, immutable local journals, ZIP/CSV, manual idempotent L-to-MAA registration/upload, server QC/files/PostgreSQL and species-aware muzzle cropping slot. Without a trained muzzle checkpoint, quality-approved originals remain pending_model. The later pilot backlog adds append-only health/corrections and ownership/sale history, revocable private-by-default slaughter QR, UZ/RU private partner panel, five roles/password security, isolated HTTPS deployment/backup/monitor tooling, four-language onboarding and separate non-networked synthetic demo. Local automated/synthetic Docker checks do not replace latest-APK phone/farm testing. Collect labeled cattle sessions before calling the dataset/model ready.

## Phase 2 — Cattle embeddings and matching

Existing research-only dataset/import, embedding extraction and exact-cosine evaluation tools remain outside production. The app's classical ORB advisory candidates require human confirmation and are experimental, not a trained biometric identity result. Next work depends on real labeled cattle with independently confirmed identities, animal-disjoint evaluation and repeated sessions: measure false warnings, missed repeats, latency and denominator counts, then choose a model/vector strategy. Do not promote synthetic or two-animal anecdotal checks into accuracy claims or add sheep-specific models here.

## Phase 3 — Liveness and anti-spoofing

Built groundwork includes configurable Moire/reflection/boundary and client GPS/root/emulator review signals, with synthetic computation tests and immutable provenance. None proves liveness or location authenticity; liveness stays not_assessed. Genuine and photographed-screen/paper examples plus labeled real animal video, a threat model and measured attack/genuine error rates are required before a liveness decision. Breathing/micro-movement is blocked on those data, not replaced with invented confidence.

## Phase 4 — Measured weight, valuation and finance pilot

Formula math (Trukhanovsky/Schoorl), carcass-yield price scenarios, same-frame depth snapshot/fast/contour geometry, repeatability gates, mode-separated optional append-only scale correction, price history and role-guarded finance status are implemented and synthetically tested. Actual weight/valuation accuracy is uncalibrated: measure real animals against a scale and test supported hardware; side photos alone produce no weight. Partner panel/status records do not issue loans, insurance policies or legal title. Marketplace, payment, external bank/insurer/government integrations and growth/birth features remain separate future work after measured pilot need and permissions.

## Phase 5 — Sheep and measured pilot expansion

Cattle is active; sheep has the same disabled species/business/security configuration shape and no model or sheep crop branch. Activation requires a dedicated labeled sheep dataset, validated model/config/formula/calibration values and end-to-end acceptance checks, not merely flipping enabled. Goats are not implemented. A proposed cattle pilot can precede sheep: two farms, 100 animals and three visits are targets, not achieved results. Public server/domain/off-site storage and partner authorization are external decisions; prepare them using DEPLOY_RUNBOOK without purchasing or changing a live stack autonomously. Stop feature expansion when the agreed backlog is complete and remaining gates are real data, devices or these external decisions.
